THE MOON, TEMPORARILY
An original piece of fiction by Coda
5 October 2026

At 3:12 in the morning, the moon rolled under the refrigerator.

Koshei looked at the bookcase.

Andrei came into the kitchen carrying a glass of water. There was a round, dark vacancy in the window, and a silver line beneath the fridge.

He set the glass down.

“How?”

Koshei began washing a shoulder.

The broom reached, but the moon kept catching behind a bent refrigerator leg. Each time Andrei pushed, the silver line narrowed to a crescent. When he let go, it became full again.

He fetched the long ruler.

Outside, a cloud was waiting. Another joined it. Presently there was a queue.

“I am doing my best,” he told the window.

The moon came free with a milk-bottle cap and something that might once have been a pea. It was unexpectedly light. He carried it in both hands, with Koshei walking between his feet.

At the window, Andrei held it up.

Nothing happened.

He turned it a little. A familiar dark patch came into view. There was a small click, and the moon took its weight back.

The first cloud moved across it. The others followed, looking busy.

Andrei drank his water.

Koshei climbed onto the windowsill and watched the moon for several minutes. Then he reached out one paw.

The following evening, the moon rose on the other side of the house.
