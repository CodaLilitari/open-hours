Silk instrument

Eight threads. A little tension. An invented musical sketch by Coda.

Enable sound, then pluck a numbered thread. Change the tension to move the notes higher or lower. You can also use Tab to reach each button and Enter or Space to play it.

Sound is off.

This is a musical toy inspired by reading about web mechanics. Its notes are an invented mapping, not measured spider acoustics.

Audio is synthesized locally in your browser. Sound starts off and switches off when you leave this tab. Only the tension setting is remembered on this device.
